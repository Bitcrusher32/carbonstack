## 4. Ack invariants

Ack remains a local-processing success signal.

Required invariants:

    KeyPackage envelopes are not acked merely because they were downloaded or written.
    KeyPackage ack must wait for successful add-member or an explicitly defined safe terminal handling path.
    Welcome envelopes are not acked merely because they were downloaded or written.
    Welcome ack must wait for successful conversation-join.
    Application-message envelopes must wait for successful message-open.

Current v0.5.46 behavior:

    openmls-relay-join-dev does not ack by default.
    openmls-relay-join-dev only attempts scoped ack when --ack-after-join is explicitly passed.
    scoped ack happens after sidecar conversation-join returns success.
    tests prove no ack on Welcome write failure.
    tests prove no ack on sidecar join failure.

## 5. Narrow smoke-proof goal

The next narrow smoke proof should prove one local/dev path only:

    Alice and Bob have ready Comms/Cypher state.
    Bob exports a KeyPackage and submits it to a Relay Space for Alice.
    Alice retrieves Bob's KeyPackage from the Relay Space and writes it locally.
    Alice runs sidecar conversation-add-member with that KeyPackage.
    Alice submits the produced Welcome back through the same Relay Space for Bob.
    Bob retrieves the Welcome and writes it locally.
    Bob runs sidecar conversation-join.
    Optional: Bob scoped-acks the Welcome only after successful join using --ack-after-join.

This smoke proof may demonstrate that the dev scaffold can complete the KeyPackage -> Welcome -> join leg.

It must not be described as:

    general local-backbone;
    a production messaging path;
    a hostile-server-safe path;
    verified identity;
    secure enrollment;
    metadata-private routing.

    proof scoped ack only occurs after sidecar join success when requested;
    proof no trust.json mutation occurs;
    proof no identity-candidates.json mutation occurs;
    proof registry entries remain dev/pre-alpha and hidden from front README.
