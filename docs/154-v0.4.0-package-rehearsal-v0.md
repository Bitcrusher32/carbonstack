## 7. Important Observation

During `full`, `release-snapshot` runs `core` before `local-cypher`.

Because `core` generates OpenMLS sidecar state and Rust build artifacts, `local-cypher` may observe known generated roots during its artifact scan when it runs after `release-snapshot`.

This was not a blocker in v0.3.36 because:

    the artifact scan classified the paths as known OpenMLS generated roots
    validation continued successfully
    --clean-generated removed the known generated roots after successful profile execution
    final artifact check showed the known roots were absent

Do not treat this as a release failure.

A future cleanup-boundary patch may be considered later, but v0.3.36 does not require it.
