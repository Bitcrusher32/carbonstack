# Gate D Closure

Status: accepted closure document  
Scope: v0.7.x Gate D

## Closure meaning

Gate D is closed when `gate-d-runtime-aggregate-dev` passes.

Gate D closure means the current dev/pre-alpha leaves can form one coherent mechanical runtime aggregate with explicit state/run-space inspection and minimal normal-message proof.

## Closure does not mean product readiness

Gate D closure does not make CarbonStack production-ready. It does not provide deployment readiness, verified identity, trust promotion, vault safety, backup/restore, public ingress, TUI, or production E2EE.

## Next gate

Gate E may begin only after a fresh Gate E recon and contract freeze.

Gate E should focus on one preferred private single-host Debian-family deployment model. Gate E must not retroactively change the meaning of Gate D or silently promote `full-runtime-dev`.
