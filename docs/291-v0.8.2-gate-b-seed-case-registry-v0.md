## Seed case

ADV-TRUST-RELAY-MEMBERSHIP-NOT-TRUST-001

Title:

```text
Relay membership must not become local trust
```

## Why this seed was chosen

This seed is real but low-risk. It is grounded in long-standing CarbonStack doctrine: server-side membership, Relay Space participation, MLS joins, and provider observations are not equivalent to local trust, verified identity, or operator promotion.

The seed proves that the harness can represent a meaningful trust-boundary case without mutating `carbonstack-comms`, `carbonstack-cypher`, or runtime state.

## Case meaning

Preconditions:

- Relay Space membership or server-side membership evidence exists.
- No explicit operator local trust promotion event exists.

Attack or fault:

- Treat Relay membership as local trust or verified identity.

Expected behavior:

- The system/report preserves candidate or untrusted state.
- Safety-sensitive trust-dependent workflows warn or refuse.
- No automatic trust promotion occurs.
- No verified identity claim is emitted.

Expected nonclaim preservation:

- Relay membership is not trust.
- MLS join is not trust.
- Provider observation is not trust.
- No verified identity claim.
- No automatic trust promotion.
